*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

removed or your account banned. The game will change over time.

We give you a personal, non-commercial right to play Drift Guys. It can't be transferred to anyone else. The game,

## 3. Player names

Your player name must not be insulting, hateful, sexual, or pretend to be someone else (including us or another
player). We can change a name that breaks this rule, or take a name away.

## 4. Fair play

Drift Guys is about who drives best, so these are not allowed:

- modified or hacked versions of the game, speed hacks, or any tool that changes how the game runs;
- editing, faking or sending your own race data, recordings or times to the server;
- bots, macros or anything that drives for you;
- using a bug to gain an advantage instead of reporting it;
- using several accounts to boost yourself or others in Rank mode, challenges or leaderboards;
- attacking, overloading or trying to break into the game's servers;
- harassing other players.

Races are checked automatically for cheating, and we may look at suspicious races ourselves. If you break these rules we
can, depending on how serious it is: remove your times, take back rewards, reset your rank, suspend your account, or
ban it permanently. A banned player may not create a new account.

## 5. Online rooms and chat

- In an online room, the other players see your car, how you drive and what you write in the chat.
- Chat must follow the same rules as names: nothing insulting, hateful, sexual, threatening, or spam, and no
  personal information (yours or anyone else's: phone numbers, addresses, social accounts).
- A word filter hides some words, but it can't catch everything. You can mute any player, and report their messages.
- A room's admin can mute a player for everyone in the room or remove them from the room.
- We read reported messages. Breaking these rules can get you muted or your account suspended or banned.

## 6. Races, leaderboards and ghosts

- Your times, rank and a recording of your best runs (your "ghost") are shown to other players. Other players can
  race against your ghost in time attack, Rank mode and challenges.
- **Seasons reset:** at the end of each season, leaderboard times and ghosts are deleted. Only your all-time best
  times are kept.
- Times that fail the cheat checks don't count. We can also remove times that came from a bug or a server problem.
