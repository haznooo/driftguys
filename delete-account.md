# Delete your Drift Guys account

## In the game (fastest)

1. Open Drift Guys and go to **Settings** on the main menu.
2. Press **Delete account**.
3. Type your player ID and press **Delete**.
4. Pick your Google account to confirm it's you.

Your account is deleted straight away.

## Without the game

Email **[contact email]** **from the email address of the Google account you play with**, with the subject
"Delete my account". Add your player ID if you know it (it's on your profile).

We'll delete the account within one month and reply when it's done. The email must come from the account's own
address, so nobody else can delete your account.

## What is deleted

- Your Google sign-in link and email address
- Your player name and profile picture
- Your cars, upgrades and nitro looks
- Your race times, ghosts and race recordings, and your rank
- Your battle pass, achievements and mail
- Your friends, friend requests and challenges
- Your sign-in records (IP addresses and technical information)
- Name reports you sent, and reports about your name
- Chat reports you sent, and reports about your chat messages
- Error reports the game sent from your phone

## What is kept

- An empty record that an account once existed.
- The history of coin and gem changes, ad rewards and purchases (if any), with **no email, Google ID or name**
  attached. It's kept for accounting and to stop fraud.
- Records of races our cheat check refused or marked as suspicious (the race, the time and why), with **no email,
  Google ID or name** attached. A suspicious race's recording is kept until we've reviewed it, then deleted. This keeps
  the game fair.

Backups that still contain your account are gone within 30 days.

## Good to know

- Deleting is permanent and can't be undone.
- Uninstalling the game does **not** delete your account.
- If you sign in again later with the same Google account, you start as a new player from zero.
